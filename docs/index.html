Car Squeeze

Your car is boxed into an impossible parking spot. Turn a real steering wheel, press the gas and reverse pedals, and squeeze it out without a scratch. The back of the car cuts inside the front, just like a real car.

Coming soon to the App Store
Real steeringA rally steering wheel on one side, gas and reverse pedals on the other. Steer while you hold a pedal.
180 tight spotsSix parking lots of 30 levels each: a supermarket, a downtown garage, Main Street, the airport, Old Town and a rooftop.
Every level can be solvedEvery spot is checked by a solver, so there is always a way out. Stuck? Watch a see-through car drive the answer.
Wheel guidesDashed lines show where your front corner and rear wheel will go, and a red box warns you before you touch something.
Daily SpotA new spot every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.
No ads, no accountNo ads, no sign-up, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.
