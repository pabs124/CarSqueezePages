Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: Car Squeeze collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Your progress and settings

Your stars, finished levels, Daily Spot streak, car colour and settings (sound, vibration, guides, parking sensor, left-handed controls) are saved on your device in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

No permissions

Car Squeeze does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission. It does not send notifications.

Purchases

The optional Full Garage unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. Car Squeeze never sees your payment details and only learns whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account. You can restore it at any time.

Children

Car Squeeze does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
