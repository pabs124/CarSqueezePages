How do I drive?

Drag around the steering wheel to turn it, like turning a real wheel. Hold the gas pedal to drive forward and the R pedal to reverse. You can steer while you hold a pedal. Double-tap the wheel to straighten it. Drive out through the green EXIT to finish the level.

What are the dashed lines?

They're the wheel guides. One line shows where your outer front corner will go, the other where your inner rear wheel will go. When a corner is about to touch something, a red box appears on it. Turn the guides on or off with the button at the top of the level, or in Settings.

What counts as a move, and how do I get three stars?

Every time you switch between driving forward and reversing, that's a new move. You get one star for getting out, one for doing it in par moves or fewer, and one for not touching anything.

Why did the round end?

Three scratches end the round. Tap Try again to start the level over. The first three practice levels never end on scratches.

I'm stuck. How do hints work?

Tap the light bulb and a see-through car drives the solution from the start. You get three free hints every day. Full Garage gives unlimited hints. Every level has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way out.

What is the Daily Spot?

A new level every day, the same for everyone. Finish it on consecutive days to build a streak. Missing one day a week won't break it.

The steering is on the wrong side for me.

Turn on Left-handed controls in Settings to put the wheel on the right and the pedals on the left.

How do I turn off the sound, vibration or parking-sensor beeps?

Open Settings from the gear on the home screen, or pause a level. Sound, Vibration and Parking sensor each have their own switch.

What does Full Garage unlock?

All six parking lots (180 levels), all eight car colours, unlimited hints and any lots added in future updates. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The Supermarket lot and the Daily Spot are free forever.

I bought Full Garage. How do I get it on another device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. If you delete the app, your stars and streak are deleted with it. Your Full Garage purchase is never lost: restore it from Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
